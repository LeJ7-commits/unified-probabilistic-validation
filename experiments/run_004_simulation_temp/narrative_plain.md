# Plain Language Governance Summary

**Model:** simulation_temp  
**Classification:** GREEN

The temperature forecasting model is performing well and meets all our risk management standards. Think of it like a weather forecast - we want it to be accurate most of the time, but not so narrow that it misses important possibilities. Our model correctly captures actual temperatures within its predicted ranges about 90% of the time, which is very close to our target. The prediction ranges are appropriately sized - not too wide (which would be unhelpful) or too narrow (which would be risky). No additional capital reserves or special reporting requirements are needed, and the model can continue operating under normal oversight procedures.
