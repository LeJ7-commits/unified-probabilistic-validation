# Governance Narrative: simulation_temp

**Classification:** GREEN

---

## Technical Summary

The Monte Carlo temperature simulation model has achieved a GREEN classification under normal market regime conditions, indicating satisfactory probabilistic performance across all validation dimensions. The model demonstrates empirical coverage of 89.6% against a 90% target, resulting in a minimal coverage error of -0.41% that falls within acceptable tolerance bands. Anfuso backtesting yields GREEN signals across all quantiles (lower, upper, and total), with breach rates of 6.3% (lower) and 4.1% (upper) that comply with Basel traffic-light thresholds. The mean pinball loss of 0.307 and mean prediction width of 9.86 units indicate acceptable sharpness-reliability trade-offs. No capital multiplier adjustments or enhanced REMIT reporting obligations are triggered under current governance frameworks.

## Plain Language Summary

The temperature forecasting model is performing well and meets all our risk management standards. Think of it like a weather forecast - we want it to be accurate most of the time, but not so narrow that it misses important possibilities. Our model correctly captures actual temperatures within its predicted ranges about 90% of the time, which is very close to our target. The prediction ranges are appropriately sized - not too wide (which would be unhelpful) or too narrow (which would be risky). No additional capital reserves or special reporting requirements are needed, and the model can continue operating under normal oversight procedures.
